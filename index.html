Benefits

Second Life Furniture d.o.o. buys used solid-wood furniture from its owners in Istria and Kvarner. Our restorers give each piece a second life in our workshop in Rijeka. You can buy in our web shop or see the pieces in our warehouse showroom in Rijeka. You decide how much of the work you want: take a piece as found and restore it yourself, collect it restored, or have it restored, delivered and assembled in the Rijeka area.

Solid wood for less than half the price of new

A comparable new solid-wood dining table costs about €1,100 in furniture stores. Ours costs €249 to €429.

Pay only for the work you want

Take a piece as found, collect it restored, or have it delivered and assembled. Restoration is €100 per piece; independent restorers charge €150 to €250.

FAQ

Why does the same piece have three prices?

Because you pay only for the work you want. The piece price is the same for everyone; restoration adds €100 and delivery with assembly in the Rijeka area adds €80.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver and assemble in the Rijeka area. You can also collect any piece from our warehouse in Rijeka.

Do prices ever go down?

Yes, in one simple way: if a piece is still for sale after 45 days, its piece price drops by 20%. Restoration and delivery prices stay the same, and any reduction is shown against the lowest price of the previous 30 days.

Is your furniture eco-friendly?

We do not use vague labels. What we can say for sure: every piece is used solid-wood furniture that we restore instead of making new.
